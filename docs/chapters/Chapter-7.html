Chapter 7: Cell Communication and Multicellularity

Signal Transduction Pathway 信号转导通路

  • a sequence of molecular events and chemical reactions that lead to a cell’s response to a signal

  • every pathway involves three elements

    • signal 信号

    • receptor 受体

    • response 响应

Types of Chemical Signals 化学信号类型

Signal Type Mode of Action Example
autocrine 自分泌 diffuse to and affect the cells that produce them tumor cells that make and respond to signals stimulating cell division
juxtacrine 领分泌/接触依赖 affect only cells right next to and in contact with the signal-producing cell common during development when cells are differentiating
paracrine 旁分泌 diffuse to and affect nearby cells inflammation signals from skin cells to nearby blood cells during healing
hormones 激素 travel through circulatory systems of animals or vascular system of plants signals that act on distant cells

Specificity 特异性

  • All cells may be exposed to a signal, but only cells with the appropriate receptors can respond.

  • Specificity depends on whether the target cell expresses the specific receptor for that signal.

Crosstalk 交叉对话

  • Interactions between different signal transduction 传导 pathways.

  • Multiple pathways may converge on a single transcription factor, allowing a single gene to be regulated by multiple signals.

  • Activation of one pathway may inhibit another.

  • Biologists often refer to “signaling networks 信号网络” rather than pathways due to this complexity.

7.2 Receptors Bind Signals to Initiate a Cellular Response

Ligand 配体

  • A specific chemical signal molecule that fits into a 3-D site on its protein receptor.

  • Ligand binding causes the receptor protein to undergo a conformational change 构想变化, initiating a cellular response.

  • The ligand itself is usually not changed; its role is purely to “knock on the door” 触发信号.

Receptor-Ligand Binding Kinetics 受体-配体结合动力学

  • binding is reversible:

\[ \ce{R + L <=> RL} \]

  • rate of binding

\[ \ce{k_1[R][L]} \]

  • rate of dissociation

\[ \ce{k_2[RL]} \]

  • at equilibrium:

\[ \ce{k_1[R][L]} = \ce{k_2[RL]} \]

  • Dissociation Constant 解离常数, \(\ce{K_D}\)

    • \[ \ce{K_D} = \frac{k_2}{k_1} = \frac{\ce{[R][L]}}{\ce{[RL]}} \]

    • Lower \(\mathrm{K_D}\) = higher affinity 亲和力高 of ligand for receptor

    • Higher \(\mathrm{K_D}\) = lower affinity 亲和力低, more ligand needed to trigger signal transduction

Agonists 激动剂 and Antagonists 拮抗剂

  • agonists 激动剂: chemicals that bind to the receptor and set it into signal transduction mode, just like the ligand

  • antagonist 拮抗剂: inhibitors that bind to the receptor and “freeze” it in place, preventing the real ligand from binding, but do not trigger signal transduction

  • Caffeine has a structure similar to adenosine 腺苷

  • acts as an antagonist at adenosine receptors

  • preventing adenosine binding and allowing continued nerve cell activity.

Receptor Types by Locations

Receptor Type Location Signals Detected Example
membrane receptors 膜受体 cell membrane large or polar ligands that cannot cross the lipid bilayer insulin receptor 胰岛素受体
intracellular receptors 胞内受体 cytoplasm or nucleus small or nonpolar ligands that can diffuse across the membrane estrogen receptor 雌激素受体
cortisol receptor 皮质醇受体

3 Categories of Cell Membrane Receptors

  1. Ion Channels 离子通道受体
  • Ligand binding causes conformational change in the channel protein, opening the gate, allowing ions (\(\ce{Na+}\), \(\ce{K+}\), \(\ce{Ca^2+}\), \(\ce{Cl-}\)) to enter or leave the cell.

  • acetylcholine receptor:

    • a ligand-gated \(\ce{Na+}\) channel located in the cell membrane of skeletal muscle cells

    • composed of 5 polypeptide subunits

    • when acetylcholine binds to 2 of the subunits, the gate opens and \(\ce{Na+}\) flows into the cell, initiating muscle contraction

  1. Protein Kinase Receptors 蛋白激酶受体

  • catalyze the phosphorylation 磷酸化 of themselves or other proteins, changing their shape and function

  • 3 amino acids can be phosphorylated: serine 丝氨酸, threonine 苏氨酸, tyrosine 酪氨酸

  • over 500 genes encode protein kinases in humans

  • insuline receptor:

    • has 2 copies of \(\alpha\) subunits and 2 copies of \(\beta\) subunits

    • when insulin binds, the receptor becomes activated and phosphorylates itself and insulin-response substrates

    • the ultimately leads to insertion of glucose transporters 葡萄糖转运蛋白 into the cell membrane

  1. G Protein-Coupled Receptors G蛋白偶联受体, GPCR

  • many roles

    • light detection (photoreceptors 光感受器)

    • odor detection (olfactory receptors 嗅觉受体)

    • regulation of mood and behavior (e.g., mating in voles)

  • the receptors for oxytocin 催产素 and vasopressin 加压素 are G protein-coupled receptors

  • structure: 3 polypeptide subunits (\(\alpha\), \(\beta\), \(\gamma\)); can bind receptor, GDP/GTP, and effector protein

  • mechanisms of aciton:

    1. Ligand binds to extracellular side of receptor \(\Longrightarrow\) receptor changes shape

    2. Cytoplasmic region exposes a site that binds to a G protein

    3. G protein binds to activated receptor; GDP is exchanged for GTP

    4. GTP-bound \(\alpha\) submit separates from the \(\beta \gamma\) subunits and diffuses in the membrane

    5. \(\alpha\) submits binds to and activates and effector protein 效应蛋白

    6. GTP is hydrolyzed to GDP \(\Longrightarrow\) \(\alpha\) subunit becomes inactive and reassembles with \(\beta \gamma\) subunits

Intracellular Receptors 胞内受体

  • located in the cytoplasm or nucleus

  • respond to physical signals (e.g., light) or chemical signals that can cross the cell membrane (e.g., steroid hormones)

  • many intracellular receptors are transcription factors 转录因子

  • normally bound to a chaperone protein 分子伴侣 that blocks it from entering the nucleus

  • cortisol binding causes the receptor to change shape, releasing the chaperone 分子伴侣

  • the receptor enters the nucleus and binds to DNA, altering gene transcription

7.3 Signal Transduction Spreads the Response to a Signal through the Cell

Protein Kinase Cascade 蛋白激酶级联反应

  • e.g., Ras signaling pathway 肾素-血管紧张素系统（renin-angiotensin system） (Ras is a G protein)

  • pathway: growth factor \(\Longrightarrow\) receptor tyrosine kinase \(\Longrightarrow\) Ras (G protein) \(\Longrightarrow\) MAPK cascade \(\Longrightarrow\) nucleus \(\Longrightarrow\) gene expression \(\Longrightarrow\) cell division

  • Ras and cancer: In some bladder cancer cells, Ras is permanently bound to GTP and constantly active, causing continuous cell division

Advantages of Protein Kinase Cascades 级联反应的优势

  1. amplification 信号放大: each activated protein kinase can phosphorylate many target proteins

  2. signal reaches the nucleus 信号传递到细胞核: membrane signals can affect gene expression

  3. specificity 特异性: multiple steps provide more regulation levels

  4. response diversity 响应多样性: different target proteins at each step can produce different effects

1 molecule of epinephrine 肾上腺素 binds to receptor on liver cell \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • activates 20 molecuels of cAMP \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • activates 20 molecules of protein kinase A \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • activates 100 molecles of phosphorylase kinase \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • activates 1,000 molecules of glycogen phosphorylase \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • produces 10,000 molecules of glucose-1-phosphate \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • releases 10,000 molecuels of blood glucose

Second Messengers 第二信使

  1. Cyclic AMP 环磷酸腺苷 cAMP

  • produced from ATP by adenylyl cyclase 腺苷酸环化酶

  • broken down by phosphodiesterase 磷酸二酯酶

  • functions: amplifies signal, distributes signal to multiple targets

  • multiple receptors can use the same cAMP pathway to produce different effects

  1. Lipid-Derived Second Messengers 脂质来源的第二信使

  • source: phosphatidyl inositol-bisphosphate 磷脂酰肌醇二磷酸 \(\ce{PIP2}\)

  • phospholipase C 磷脂酶 C, hydrolyzes \(\ce{PIP2}\) to produce:

    • \(\ce{IP3}\) 肌醇三磷酸, inositol trisphosphate: released into the cytoplasm; acts on \(\ce{Ca^2+}\) channels in the ER membrane

    • DAG 二酰甘油, diacylglycerol: remains in the cell membrane

  • \(\ce{IP3}\) and DAG work together to activate Protein Kinase C 蛋白激酶 C, PKC

  1. Calcium Ions 钙离子 \(\ce{Ca^2+}\)

  • cytosolic concentration: ~\(\pu{0.1 \mu M}\) (very low)

  • extracellular and ER concentrations: much higher

  • active transport pumps maintian this concentration difference

  • signals (e.g., \(\ce{IP3}\)) can open \(\ce{Ca^2+}\) channels, causing cytosolic \(\ce{Ca^2+}\) to increase up to 100-fold within a fraction of a second

  • \(\ce{Ca^2+}\) activates PKC, controls other ion channels, stimulates secretion by exocytosis

  • Fertilization in a sea star egg - sperm entry causes a rush of \(\ce{Ca^2+}\) from the environment into the cytoplasm, triggering cell division and development :::

  1. Nitric Oxide 一氧化氮 \(\ce{NO}\)

  • a gas that acts as a second messenger

  • produced from arginine 精氨酸 by \(\ce{NO}\) synthase (\(\ce{NO}\) 合酶)

  • diffuses to target cells

    • acetylcholine \(\Longrightarrow\)

    • \(\ce{IP3}\) release \(\Longrightarrow\)

    • \(\ce{Ca^2+}\) channel opening \(\Longrightarrow\)

    • \(\ce{Ca^2+}\) increase \(\Longrightarrow\)

    • \(\ce{NO}\) synthase activation \(\Longrightarrow\)

    • \(\ce{NO}\) production \(\Longrightarrow\)

    • \(\ce{NO}\) diffuses to smooth muscle cells \(\Longrightarrow\)

    • activates guanylyl cylase 鸟苷酸环化酶 \(\Longrightarrow\)

    • produces cGMP \(\Longrightarrow\)

    • muscle relaxation

  • drug applications:

    • nitroglycerin 硝酸甘油: releases \(\ce{NO}\), dilates blood vessels, treats angina

    • sildenafil 西地那非, Viagra: inhibits phosphodiesterase that breaks down cGMP, enhancing \(\ce{NO}\) signaling, causing relaxation of smooth muscles in the penis

Regulation of Signal Transduction 信号传导的调控

Cells can regulate signal transduction by:

  1. synthesis or breakdown of enzymes: e.g., increasing adenylyl cyclase 腺苷酸环化酶 synthesis or decreasing phosphodiesterase 磷酸二酯酶 breakdown will raise cAMP levels

  2. activation or inhibition of enzymes by other molecules: e.g., sidenafil inhibits cGMP phosphodiesterase 磷酸二酯酶

  3. ion concentration regulation: e.g., membrane pumps and ion channels regulate \(\ce{Ca^2+}\) concentration

7.4 Cells Change in Response to Signals in Several Ways

Three Primary Forms of Cellular Response

  1. opening or closing of ion channels

  2. changes in enzyme activities - activation or inhibition

  3. differential gene expression - activation or inhibition of transcription

Ion Channel Responses

Oxytocin 催产素 signaling pathway:

  • oxytocin \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • GPCR \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • G protein activation \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • \(\ce{IP3-DAG}\) pathway \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • \(\ce{Ca^2+}\) channel opening \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • \(\ce{Na+}\) channel opening \(\Longrightarrow\)

  • increased nerve cell activity

  • In prairie voles: oxytocin induces bonding and parental care behaviors

  • In humans: oxytocin is involved in trusting behavior (Paul Zak’s experiments)

Enzyme Activity Changes

  • enzymes are modified covalently (phosphorylation) or noncovalently (cAMP binding)

  • Shape changes expose or hide active sites

Effect Enzyme Action Result
inhibition 抑制 glycogen synthase 糖原合酶 phosphorylated and inactivated by protein kinase A prevents glucose from being stored as glycogen
activation 激活 phosphorylase kinase 磷酸化酶激酶 phosphorylated and activated by protein kinase A activates glycogen phosphorylase \(\Longrightarrow\) releases glucose from glycogen

Gene Expression Changes

  • common targets of signal transduction are transcription factors 转录因子

  • transcription factors bind to specific DNA sequences and activate or inactivate transcription of adjacent genes

  • e.g., MAPK enters the nucleus and phosphorylates a protein that stimulates expression of genes involved in cell proliferation

7.5 Adjacent Cells in a Multicellular Organism Can Communicate Directly

Gap Junctions 间隙连接 - Animal Cells

  • channels between adjacent animal cells

  • can oppupy up to 25% of the cell membrane area

  • structure: connexons 连接子 composed of six subunits of the protein connesin 连接蛋白

  • two connexons from adjacent cells come together to form a channel linking the cytoplasms

  • channel pore diameter: ~ \(\pu{1.5nm}\)

  • permeability: small molecules and ions (too narrow for large molecules)

  • function: hormones and second messengers can move through gap junctions; allows coordinated responses by all cells in a tissue

  • e.g., lens of mammalian eye - only cells at the periphery are close to blood supply; gap junctions allow nutrients and wastes to diffuse rapidly through all lens cells

Plasmodesmata 胞间连丝 - Plant Cells

  • membrane-lined tunnels that traverse the thick cell walls separating plant cells

  • a typical plant cell has several thousand plasmodesmata

  • structure:

    • lined by fused cell membranes themselves (not by separate protein channels)

    • contains desmotubule 连丝微管 derived from the endoplasmic reticulum, filling most of the opening

    • effective pore size for diffusion: ~ \(\pu{1.5nm}\)

  • permeability: small metabolites and ions

  • special feature: some viruses can move through plasmodesmata using “movement proteins”

  • importance: plants lack capilaries; rely on plasmodesmata for rapid diffusion of hormones from production site to action site

Comparison: Gap Junction vs. Plasmodesmata

Feature Gap Junctions (Animals) Plasmodesmata (Plants)
Location between adjacent animal cells between adjacent plant cells
structure connexons made of connexin protein fused cell membranes + desmotubule
pore diameter ~ \(\pu{1.5nm}\) ~ \(\pu{1.5nm}\) (effective)
permeability small molecules, ions small metabolites ions
special features hundreds of channels per cell desmotubule fills most of the space

Evolution of Multicellularity 多细胞性的进化 - Volvocine Green Algae 团藻目绿藻

Proposed Steps in Evolution of Multicellularity 多细胞性进化的可能步骤

  1. Aggregation of cells into a cluster 细胞聚集成团

  2. Intercellular communicaiton within the cluster 团内细胞间通讯

  3. Specializaiton of some cells within the cluster 部分细胞特化

  4. Organization of specialized cells into tissues 特化细胞组织化为组织

no cell specialization		 limited

Key Observation

  • In Gonium, Pandorina, Eudoring, and Pleodoring, a single detached cell can divide and form a new colony.

  • In Volvox, most cells (especially reproductive cells) have lost the ability to live and divide independently

    • they are now inextricably part of the organism

  • The separation of somatic and reproductive functions, and the loss of cellular independence, is made possible by intercellular signaling mechanisms

Investigating Life: Is Oxytocin 催产素 a “Trust” Signal in Humans

Paul Zak’s Experiments

  • hypothesis: oxytocin is involved in trust between humans

  • method: economics trust game experiment - money transfers between strangers

  • results:

    • recipients who felt trusted by donor not only returned more money but also has elevated oxytocin levels

    • donors given oxytocin nasal spray donated an average of 9.6 units vs. 8.1 units in controls (significant difference)

    • when donors were told exactly how much to give (removing donor intent), oxytocin has no effect \(\Longrightarrow\) indicating the trust relationship requires intentional generosity

  • conclusion: oxytocin correlates with trusting behavior in humans

Further Applicaitons

  • Oxytocin is also released during pleasant activites such as sexual interactions, massage, and hugs.

  • Oxytocin and other behavior-modulating peptides are being investigated as a treatment for autism 自闭症.

  • Commercial products (e.g., “Liquid Trust”) market oxytocin as a “trust” fragrance.

  • The book Make Love Like a Prairie Vole offers steps to a happier love life based on these findings.
