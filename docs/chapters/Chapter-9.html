Chapter 9: Pathways That Harvest Chemical Energy

9.1 Cells Harvest Chemical Energy for Glucose Oxidation
细胞从葡萄糖氧化中收获化学能

Key Principles of Metabolic Pathways
代谢通路的五个基本原则

  1. A complex chemical transformation occurs in a series of separate reactions that form a metabolic pathway.
    复杂化学转化通过一系列独立反应完成

  2. Each reaction is catalyzed by a specific enzyme.
    每步反应由特定酶催化

  3. Many metabolic pathways are similar in all organisms, from bacteria to humans.
    从细菌到人类, 许多通路高度保守

  4. In eukaryotes, many metabolic pathways are compartmentalized within specifc organelles.
    真核生物中, 许多通路被区隔在细胞器中

  5. Some key enzymes can be inhibited or activated to alter the rate of the pathway.
    关键酶可被抑制或激活以调控通路速率

Complete Oxidation of Glucose

  • Equation

\[ \ce{C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O + free energy ( \Delta \mathrm{G} = \pu{-686kcal/mol})} \]

  • This is a redox reaction: glucose is oxidized (loses \(\ce{e-}\)), \(\ce{O2}\) is reduced (gains \(\ce{e-}\)).

Three Catabolic Processes for Glucose

  • glycolysis 糖酵解:

    • glucose \(\Longrightarrow\) two pyruvate

    • small ATP

    • anaerobic 无氧

  • cellular respiration 细胞呼吸:

    • aerobic 有氧

    • fully oxidizes pyruvate to \(\ce{CO2}\) 彻底氧化

    • large ATP

  • fermentation 发酵:

    • anaerobic 无氧

    • incomplete oxidation 不完全氧化

    • low ATP yield 能量产率低

Redox Reactions and Energy

  • oxidation: loss of \(\ce{e-}\)

  • reduction: gain of \(\ce{e-}\)

  • \(\ce{NAD+}\) is a key \(\ce{e-}\) carrier, reduced to \(\ce{NADH}\) to store energy.

  • oxidation of \(\ce{NADH}\) releases energy:

\[ \ce{NADH + H+ + 1/2 O2 -> NAD+ + H2O} ( \Delta \mathrm{G}^\circ = \pu{-52.4kcal/mol}) \]

Locations of Pathways

Pathway 通路 Eukaryotes 真核 Prokaryotes 原核
glycolysis cytoplasm cytoplasm
fermentation cytoplasm cytoplasm
citric acid cycle mitochondrial matrix cell membrane
pyruvate oxidation mitochondrial matrix cell membrane
respiratory chain inner mitochondrial membrane cell membrane

9.2 In the Presence of Oxygen, Glucose Is Fully Oxidized
有氧条件下葡萄糖被完全氧化

Glycolysis 糖酵解

  • location: cytoplasm

  • 10 enzyme-catalyzed steps 10 步酶促反应

  • 2 stages:

    • energy-investment (uses 2 \(\ce{ATP}\))

    • energy-harvesting (produces 4 \(\ce{ATP}\), 2 \(\ce{NADH}\))

  • net yield 净产: \(\ce{2 ATP + 2NADH + 2 pyruvate}\)

Pyruvate Oxidation 丙酮酸氧化

  • location: mitochondrial matrix (eukaryotes)

\[ \ce{pyruvate -> acetyl CoA + CO2 + NADH} \] \(\ce{丙酮酸 -> 乙酰 CoA + CO2 + NADH}\)

  • catalyzed by pyruvate dehydrogenase complex 由丙酮酸脱氢酶复合体催化

  • connects glycolysis to the citric acid cycle

Citric Acid Cycle 柠檬酸循环 (Krebs cycle / TCA cycle)

  • location: mitochondrial matrix

  • 8 steps: starting with

\[ \ce{acetyl CoA + oxaloacetate -> citrate} \]

  • per glucose: cycle runs twice (once per pyruvate)

  • outputs per turn:

    • \(\ce{3NADH}\)

    • \(\ce{1FADH2}\)

    • \(\ce{1GTP (-> ATP)}\)

    • \(\ce{2CO2}\)

Role of Oxygen

  • \(\ce{O2}\) is the final electron acceptor, reduced to \(\ce{H2O}\)

  • without \(\ce{O2}\), \(\ce{NADH}\) and \(\ce{FADH2}\) cannot be reoxidized, and the cycle stops

9.3 Oxidative Phosphorylation Forms ATP
氧化磷酸化生成 ATP

Two Components of Oxidative Phosphorylation

  1. electron transport 电子传递:
  • electrons from \(\ce{NADH}\)/\(\ce{FADH2}\) pass through respiratory chain, pumping \(\ce{H+}\) out of matrix
  1. chemiosmosis 化学渗透:
  • \(\ce{H+}\) flows back through \(\ce{ATP}\) synthase, driving \(\ce{ATP}\) synthesis

Respiratory Chain Complexes

Complex 复合体 Function
I \(\ce{NADH-Q reductase}\) \(\ce{NADH -> Q}\), pumps \(\ce{H+}\)
II (succinate dehydrogenase) \(\ce{FADH2 -> Q}\) (不泵 \(\ce{H+}\))
III (cytochrome c reductase) \(\ce{Q -> cytochrome c}\), pumps \(\ce{H+}\)
IV (cytochrome c oxidase) \(\ce{cytochrome c -> O2}\), pumps \(\ce{H+}\)

  • Q (ubiquinone): mobile lipid carrier in membrane 膜内可移动脂质载体

  • cytochrome c: peripheral protein in intermembrane space 膜间隙外周蛋白

Proton-Motive Force 质子驱动力

  • \(\ce{H+}\) gradient (concentration + charge) across inner mitochondrial membrane

  • \(\sim\ce{20H+}\) pumped per 4 \(\ce{e-}\)

  • energy stored in gradient used by ATP synthase

ATP Yield

  • \(\ce{NADH -> \sim 2.5ATP}\)

  • \(\ce{FADH2 -> \sim 1.5ATP}\)

ATP Synthase

  • \(\ce{F_0}\) unit: \(\ce{H+}\) channel in membrane

  • \(\ce{F_1}\) unit: catalytic sites for ATP synthesis

  • rotates like a molecular motor

  • up to 100 ATP per second

Uncoupling 解偶联

  • UCP1 in brown fat makes inner membrane permeable to \(\ce{H+}\) \(\Longrightarrow\) heat instead of ATP
    棕色脂肪中的 UCP1 使内膜对 \(\ce{H+}\) 通透 \(\Longrightarrow\) 产热而非 ATP

  • dinitrophenol is an artificial uncoupler (dangerous side effects)
    二硝基苯酚是人工解偶联剂, 副作用严重

Terminal \(\ce{e-}\) acceptor Product Example Organism

9.4 In the Absence of Oxygen, Some Energy Is Harvested from Glucose

Purpose of Fermentation

  • regenerate \(\ce{NAD+}\) from \(\ce{NADH}\) so glycolysis can continue

  • does NOT produce \(\ce{ATP}\) directly

Two Major Fermentation Pathways

  1. Lactic Acid Fermentation 乳酸发酵
  • \(\ce{丙酮酸 -> 乳酸}\)

\[ \ce{Pyruvate -> Lactate} \] \[ \ce{NADH + H+ + 1/2 O2 -> NAD+ + H2O} \]

  • occurs in muscle during intense exercise

  • lactate build-up lowers pH \(\Longrightarrow\) muscle fatigue

  1. Alcoholic Fermentation 酒精发酵
  • \(\ce{丙酮酸 -> 乙醛 + CO2}\)

\[ \ce{pyruvate -> acetaldehyde + CO2} \]

  • \(\ce{乙醛 -> 乙醇}\), 消耗 \(\ce{NADH}\)

\[ \ce{acetaldehyde -> ethanol (using NADH)} \]

  • occurs in yeast and some plant cells

Energy Yield Comparison

Process \(\ce{ATP}\) yield per glucose
glycolysis + fermentation \(\ce{\sim 2 ATP}\)
cellular respiration (full) \(\ce{\sim 32 ATP}\)

  • fermentation partially oxidizes glucose

  • much energy remains in ethanol/lactate

  • 发酵不完全氧化葡萄糖, 大量能量仍留在乙醇/乳酸中

9.5 Metabolic Pathways Are Interrelated and Regulated
代谢通路相互关联且受调控

Catabolic Interconversions
分解代谢的相互转化

Macromolecule Breakdown Product Entry point into pathways
polysaccharides 多糖 glucose glycolysis 糖酵解
lipids 脂类 \(\ce{glycerol -> DHAP}\)
\(\ce{fatty acid -> acetyle CoA\) (\(\beta\)-oxidation)		 glycolysis / citric acid cycle
proteins amino acids glycolysis or citric acid cycle (at various points)

  • \(\beta\)-oxidation: \(\ce{C15 fatty acid -> 8 acetyl CoA}\) 16碳脂肪酸 \(\longrightarrow\) 8个乙酰 CoA

  • amino acid glutamate \(\longrightarrow\) \(\alpha\)-ketoglutarate (citric acid cycle intermediate)
    谷氨酸 \(\longrightarrow\) \(\alpha\)-酮戊wu4二酸

    • produce \(\ce{NH4+}\) (toxic) \(\longrightarrow\) converted to urea for excretion
      产生有毒 \(\ce{NH4+}\) \(\longrightarrow\) 转化为尿素排出

Anabolic Interconversions
合成代谢的相互转化

  • gluconeogenesis 糖异生

    • pyruvate and other intermediates \(\longrightarrow\) glucose
      丙酮酸等中间产物 \(\longrightarrow\) 葡萄糖

  • Acetyl CoA \(\longrightarrow\) fatty acids (even-carbon chains: \(\ce{C14}\), \(\ce{C16}\), \(\ce{C18}\))
    乙酰 CoA \(\longrightarrow\) 脂肪酸, 偶数碳链

  • Citric acid cycle intermediates \(\longrightarrow\) amino acids, nucleotides, chlorophyll
    柠檬酸循环中间产物 \(\longrightarrow\) 氨基酸, 核苷酸, 叶绿素

Regulation of Pathways

Mechanisms

  1. changing enzyme amount

  2. covalent modification

  3. feedback inhibition

  4. substrate availability

Key Regulatory Enzymes

  • phosphofructokinase (PFK) in glycolysis 糖酵解中的 PFK:

    • inhibited by: ATP, citrate

    • activated by: ADP, AMP

  • isocitrate dehydrogenase in citric acid cycle

    • inhibited by: ATP, NADH

    • activated by: ADP, \(\ce{NAD+}\), isocitrate 异柠檬酸

  • excess acetyl CoA \(\longrightarrow\) diverted to fatty acid synthesis when ATP is high
    ATP 高时, 过量乙酰 CoA 转向合成脂肪酸

Investigating Life: Mitochondria and Obesity
线粒体与肥胖

  • brown fat has high mitochondria and UCP1

  • UCP1 uncouples \(\ce{e-}\) transport from ATP synthesis \(\longrightarrow\) heat production

  • mice with more UCP1 are leaner 更瘦

  • human UCP1 variants correlate with fat accumulation with age

  • activating brown fat is a potential obesity treatment strategy
