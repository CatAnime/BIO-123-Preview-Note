Chapter 8: Energy, Enzymes, and Metabolism

8.1 Chemical Transformations Involve Energy and Energy Transfers

  • types of energy

    • potential energy:

      • stored energy

      • e.g., in covalent bonds

      • e.g., concentration gradients

    • kinetic energy:

      • energy of movememt

      • does work

  • metabolism 新陈代谢

    • anabolism 合成代谢:

      • builds larger molecules

      • requires energy

      • endergonic 吸能的, \(+ \Delta \mathrm{G}\)

    • catabolism 分解代谢

      • breaks down larger molecules

      • releases energy

      • exergonic 放能的, \(- \Delta \mathrm{G}\)

  • Laws of Thermodynamics 热力学定律

    • First Law of Thermodynamics

      • Energy is neither created nor destroyed 能量守恒, only converted

    • Second Law of Thermodynamics

      • Energy conversions increase entropy 熵 (S) 无序度增加

      • some energy becomes unusable

  • Free Energy (G) 自由能

    • \[ \Delta \mathrm{G} = \Delta \mathrm{H} - \mathrm{T} \Delta \mathrm{S}\]

    • \(\Delta \mathrm{G} < 0\): exergonic 放能

      • releases energy, spontaneous

    • \(\Delta \mathrm{G} > 0\): endergonic 吸能

      • requires energy input

    • \(\Delta \mathrm{G} = 0\): at equilibrium

      • no net change

  • Equilibrium 平衡

    • forward and reverse reactions occur at same rate

    • product-to-reactant ratio is fixed

8.2 ATP Plays a Key Role in Biochemical Energetics

  • Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP) 三磷酸腺苷

    • energy currency of the cell

    • structure:\[\text{adenine 腺嘌呤} + \text{ribose 核糖} + 3 \text{phosphate groups 磷酸基团}\]

  • ATP Hydrolysis ATP 水解

    • \[\ce{ATP + H2O -> ADP + P_i + free energy}\]

    • \(\Delta \mathrm{G} \approx \pu{-7.3kcal/mol}\) \((\pu{-30kJ/mol})\), highly exergonic

    • Why so much energy?

      1. Negative charges repel - bond is high-energy

      2. Products (\(\ce{ADP + P_i}\)) are more stable

  • Energy Coupling 能量偶联

    • ATP hydrolysis drives endergonic reactions

    • e.g.,\[\ce{Glucose + ATP -> Glucose-6-phosphate +ADP}\] 葡萄糖磷酸化

    • Cycle:\[\ce{Exergonic reactions -> make ATP}\]\[\ce{ATP -> fuels endergonic reactions}\]

8.3 Enzymes Speed Up Biochemical Transformations

  • catalyst

    • speeds up reaction without being permanently changed

    • 加速反应, 自身不被永久改变

  • enzymes

    • mostly proteins

    • highly specific

      • each enzyme binds specific substrate(s) 底物

    • active site

      • where substrate binds and reaction occurs

  • activation energy 活化能, \(\mathrm{E_a}\):

    • energy needed to reach transition state 过渡态

    • enzymes lower \(\mathrm{E_a}\)

      • speed up reaction

    • important: enzymes do NOT change \(\Delta \mathrm{G}\) or equilibrium

  • enzyme-substrate complex (ES) 酶-底物复合物:

    • \[\ce{E +S <=> ES -> E + P}\]

  • enzyme classes:

Name Functions
oxidoreductases 氧化还原酶 transfer electrons
transferases 转移酶 transfer funcitonal groups 转移功能基团
hydrolases 水解酶 add water to break bonds 加水断键
lyases 裂解酶 non-hydrolytic bond breakage 非水解性断键
isomerases 异构酶 rearrange atoms within molecule 分子内重排
ligases 连接酶 join molecules together

8.4 Enzymes Bring Substrates Together so Reactions Readily Occur
酶将底物聚在一区, 使反应易于进行

How Enzymes Lower \(\ce{E_a}\)

  1. orientation 定向
  • holds substrates in correct position
  1. inducing strain 诱导应变
  • stretches bonds to make them unstable
  1. chemical catalysis

  • acid-base catalysis 酸碱催化

    • tranfer \(\ce{H+}\)

  • covalent catalysis 共价催化

    • forms temporary covalent bond

  • metal ion catalysis 金属离子催化

    • use cofactors like \(\ce{Fe^2+}\), \(\ce{Zn^2+}\)

Induced Fit 诱导契合

  • enzyme changes shape upon substrate binding

  • e.g., hexokinase 已糖激酶 changes shape to exclude water 排除水分子

Non-protein Helpers 非蛋白辅助因子

  • prosthetic groups 辅基

    • permanently bound

    • e.g., heme 血红素

    • e.g., FAD

  • cofactors 辅因子

    • inorganic ions

    • e.g., \(\ce{Zn^2+}\), \(\ce{Fe^2+}\)

  • coenzymes 辅酶

    • organic molecules

    • temporarily bind

    • e.g., \(\ce{NAD+}\), \(\ce{CoA}\)

Substrate Concentration Effect

  • rate increases with [substrate], then plateaus 平台期, when enzyme is saturated

    • all active sites occupied 所有活性点位被占据

  • turnover number 转换数

    • max substrates converted per enzyme per second 每个酶每秒最多转化的底物数

8.5 Enzyme Activities Can Be Regulated 酶的活性可以被调控

Two Ways to Regulate Enzyme Activities

  1. gene expression
  • make more or less enzyme
  1. enzyme activity
  • change enzyme shape or block active site

Inhibitors 抑制剂

  • irreversible

    • covalently binds

    • permanently inactivates

    • e.g., Aspirin 阿司匹林: acetylates COX enzyme 乙酰化COS酶

  • reversible

    • competitive

      • binds active site, competes with substrate
      • overcome by more substrate

    • uncompetitive

      • binds only to ES complex

    • noncompetitive

      • binds elsewhere, changes enzyme shape

Allosteric Regulation 别构调控

  • regulator binds allosteric site 别构位点 (not active site)

  • stabilizes active or inactive form

  • often enzymes with quaternary structure 四级结构

  • sigmoidal curve S形曲线

    • sensitive to small changes in substrate 对底物微笑变化敏感

Feedback Inhibition 反馈抑制

  • end product inhibits the commitment step 承诺步骤/第一步骤

  • prevents wasting energy

  • e.g., prine synthesis 嘌呤合成, cholesterol synthesis 胆固醇合成, target of statins 他汀类药物靶点

Reversible Phosphorylation 可逆磷酸化

  • protein kinase 蛋白激酶

    • adds phosphate - activates

  • protein phosphatase

    • removes phosphate - inactivates

Environment Effects

  • pH

    • each enzyme has optimal pH

    • changes in pH alter ionization and shape

  • temperature

    • each enzyme has optimal temperature

    • too high denatures (loses 3D structure)
